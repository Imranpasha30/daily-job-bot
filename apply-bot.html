Apply Bot

Fills a job application form, shows you exactly what it filled, and submits only after you review and click. Works on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and SmartRecruiters forms, which is where 283 of your listed jobs live.

Install it once

  1. Show your browser's bookmarks bar: Ctrl + Shift + B.
  2. Drag this button onto the bookmarks bar:

Apply Bot

If dragging does not work, right-click the bookmarks bar, choose "Add page", put anything in the name, and paste the code from the box below into the URL field.

Use it on every application

  1. Open a job from your list and click Apply on the company's site.
  2. On the application form, click the Apply Bot bookmark.
  3. Press 1 — Fill this form. It fills your details, picks the safe answers on yes/no questions, and drops prepared text into the written ones.
  4. Read the table it shows you. Every filled field is listed with its value.
  5. Attach your resume by hand. Browsers do not let scripts touch file inputs, and no tool can get around that.
  6. Press 2 — I reviewed it, submit now and confirm.
The one thing it will never do for you. It does not answer yes/no questions about technologies you have not used. If a form asks about Debezium, PeerDB, Kafka, Spark, Kubernetes, Terraform, Go, Java or Scala, it stops and shows the question in red. You have no production experience with any of them, and a false tick there is caught in the first technical round. Answer those yourself, honestly.
Why it does not submit on its own. Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby all prohibit automated submission, and being flagged can lock your email out of applications at most of these companies at once. One click per application keeps you inside the rules and gives you the chance to catch a wrong answer before it is permanent.

When the bot hits something it cannot answer, it lists the question and gives you a copy unanswered button. Paste that into Claude and you will get an answer written from your real experience, which you then paste back into the form.

The honest limits

  • Resume upload is manual. A browser will not let any script set a file input. Nothing can change that.
  • "Why this company" is left blank on purpose. It is the one answer that has to be specific, and a generic one is obvious to the reader.
  • Some forms need an account first. Workday and custom portals are not supported; the bot covers the four ATS platforms your list actually uses.
  • CAPTCHAs stop everything. Solve it yourself, then press fill.
  • Check the table before you submit. Label detection is heuristic. It is right most of the time, not always, and you are the one signing the application.
